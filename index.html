Welcome to Ghana Building Contractors

27 Years Experience

Ghana Building Contractors Co. Ltd, founded in 1996, is a leading construction company in Tanzania, renowned for its expertise in civil works, building renovations, and infrastructure development. Headquartered in Dodoma, we have successfully completed projects for esteemed clients such as CRDB Bank, NMB Bank, PSSSF, and TANROADS.

Our services range from water schemes and road maintenance to specialized bank fit-outs and commercial construction. Supported by a skilled team of engineers, project managers, and technical staff, we are committed to delivering excellence through innovative solutions, strict quality standards, and sustainable practices. Ghana Building Contractors stands as a trusted partner in building Tanzania’s future.

Frequently Asked Question

You May Ask

What services does your company offer?
We provide civil works, building construction, renovations, fit-outs, and infrastructure development.
Who are your main clients?
Our clients include CRDB Bank, NMB Bank, PSSSF, TANROADS, and local government councils.
Where is your company located?
Our head office is in Dodoma, with a branch in Dar es Salaam.
When was your company founded?
We were established as a limited company in 1996.
Are you licensed and certified?
Yes, we are registered with the Contractors Registration Board (CRB) in Class Five for both building and civil works.
Do you handle large-scale projects?
Yes, we have successfully managed large-scale projects across Tanzania.
What types of infrastructure projects does your company specialize in?
We specialize in water scheme rehabilitations, road maintenance, commercial building constructions, and residential projects.
Is Ghana Building Contractors equipped to handle multi-location projects?
Yes, with branches in Dodoma and Dar es Salaam, our team is equipped for multi-site management and coordination, ensuring quality across locations.
What sets your company apart?
Our experienced team, strict quality standards, and commitment to client satisfaction make us a trusted partner.
Do you use sustainable practices?
Yes, we incorporate sustainable methods and materials wherever possible.
